Feature-Conditioned Affine Lifting of QAOA Parameters Across Circuit Depths

This study evaluates a regularized affine lift from an authenticated depth-one QAOA reference and graph descriptors to a depth-two schedule.

On collision-free feature-signature folds, mean relative regret is 0.774%, compared with 4.050% for a schedule-only affine map and 6.632% for interpolation. Source-held-out folds produce a different ranking, which is retained as a stated limit of the result.

The data comprise 144 ten-vertex neighborhoods from six topology-unique Stanford Gset graphs. The package verifies source hashes, excludes duplicate topologies, regenerates all tables and figures, and checks the angle-to-energy bound for every prediction.
